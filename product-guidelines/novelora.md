## 1. Overview
Novelora is Kontyra's professional authoring canvas and worldbuilding lore studio. It equips writers with distraction-free rich text drafting (TipTap), multi-arc timeline scheduling, and lore entity compilation.

- **TipTap Distraction-Free Editor:** Minimalist writing canvas with real-time word counts and focus mode.
- **Lore Bible Engine:** Structured database tracking Characters, Locations, Items, and Lore Events.
- **Twin Product Cross-Bridge:** Persistent `"Read on REASTUDIA"` action pill.
