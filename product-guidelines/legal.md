# Kontyra Governance, Compliance & Legal Standard

> **Official Kontyra Brand Specification & Product Division Guidelines**  
> Division: **Regulatory Governance, Privacy Policies & Terms of Service**  
> Canonical URL: `legal.kontyra.name.ng`  
> Brand Accent: **Governance Silver (`#A1A1AA`)**

---

## 1. Product Mission & Vision

The Legal Portal provides transparent, binding, and developer-friendly documentation of user rights, data protection protocols, cookie policies, service level agreements (SLA), and acceptable use standards.

---

## 2. Core Policies & Canonical Targets

- **Privacy Policy:** `https://legal.kontyra.name.ng/privacy`
- **Terms of Service:** `https://legal.kontyra.name.ng/terms`
- **Cookie Policy:** `https://legal.kontyra.name.ng/cookies`
- **Acceptable Use Policy:** `https://legal.kontyra.name.ng/acceptable-use`
- **Copyright & DMCA Notice:** `https://legal.kontyra.name.ng/copyright`

---

## 3. Formatting Standards

- **Plain-English Summaries:** Every legal clause features an accompanying callout box breaking down what it means in simple developer terms.
- **Version Badges:** Transparent versioning and last updated timestamps on all policies.
- **Copy License / Raw Text:** One-click copy for enterprise vendor assessment and legal review.
