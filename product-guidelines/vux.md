## 1. Product Mission & Vision

VUX Events is the unified ticketing and attendance verification platform for developer conferences, tech meetups, hackathons, and literary salons hosted across Africa and globally by the Kontyra community.

---

## 2. Core Functional Specifications

1. **Cryptographic Dynamic QR Tickets:**
   - Time-based rotating QR code prevents ticket screenshot sharing and secondary black-market scalping.
2. **Door Scanner PWA:**
   - Ultra-fast offline-first mobile web scanner for venue check-in verification.
3. **Multi-Tier Seat Management:**
   - Early Bird, General Admission, VIP Backstage, Student Builder passes.
4. **Instant DevOS Wallet Sync:**
   - Claimed tickets reflect immediately in the attendee's Kontyra universal profile.
