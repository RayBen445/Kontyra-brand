## 1. Product Mission & Vision

The Kontyra Blog publishes in-depth engineering post-mortems, architectural deep-dives, major feature announcements, and literary perspectives from the Kontyra founding team.

---

## 2. Editorial Features

1. **Integrated Audio Reader:** Built-in Text-To-Speech (TTS) audio player with playback speed controls (1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x) for listening on the go.
2. **Estimated Reading Time:** Automatically computed metrics displayed in the article byline.
3. **Structured Social Cards:** Dynamic OpenGraph image generation with article title, author avatar, category tag, and publication date.
4. **Interactive Salons:** Threaded comment discussion powered by Kontyra Identity SSO.
