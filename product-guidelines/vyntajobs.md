## 1. Product Mission & Vision

VyntaJobs connects vetted software engineers, designers, and technical authors with high-impact remote contracts, featuring cryptographic milestone escrow and verified DevOS project portfolio badges.

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules

1. **Milestone Escrow Contracts:**
   - Client funds are locked securely prior to milestone commencement and released automatically upon verifiable deliverable approval.
2. **Verified DevOS Portfolio Integration:**
   - Candidate proposals link directly to verified DevOS project executions and live deployments (`username.devos.kontyra.name.ng`).
3. **Dispute Resolution & Audit Chamber:**
   - Transparent mediation room logging contract revisions, submitted zip archives, and client feedback.
