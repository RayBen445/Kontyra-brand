# Kontyra Logo System & Guidelines

## 1. Overview
The Kontyra brand mark represents the interlocking continuity of intelligence and modular systems. The geometric dual-loop structure must remain strictly preserved across all media.

## 2. Approved Variants

### A. Primary Gradient Logo (`kontyra-gradient.svg`)
- **Use:** Main website hero branding, marketing materials, keynote presentations, official announcements.
- **Backgrounds:** Deep dark surfaces (`#000000`, `#050505`).

### B. White Monochrome Logo (`kontyra-white.svg`, `kontyra-white-icon.svg`)
- **Use:** Global navigation bars, dark product dashboards, code editors, CLI displays.
- **Backgrounds:** Any dark background with high contrast (`#000000` to `#18181b`).

### C. Black Monochrome Logo (`kontyra-black.svg`, `kontyra-black-icon.svg`)
- **Use:** Formal white paper print, legal documentation, light mode enterprise dashboards.
- **Backgrounds:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) or light paper stock.

### D. Favicon & App Icons (`favicon.svg`)
- **Use:** Browser tab icon, bookmark bar, PWA manifest, desktop shortcut icons.

## 3. Clear Space Rules
Maintain a minimum clear space equal to `0.5x` the icon height on all sides. No text or graphic elements may infringe upon this clearance perimeter.

## 4. Unapproved Usage
- Do not stretch, skew, or alter vector path coordinates.
- Do not apply unapproved secondary drop shadows or outer blurs.
- Do not invert gradient stop directions without brand architectural review.
